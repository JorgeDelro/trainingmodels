# Use of AI assistance

This file discloses how generative AI is used in the development of
trainingmodels. It is updated at each release.

- **Design decisions, scope and domain choices** are made by the author
  (Jorge del Rosario Fernández Santos). They are recorded in
  [DECISIONS.md](https://jorgedelro.github.io/trainingmodels/DECISIONS.md).
- **Specifications and reviews** are drafted with Claude (Anthropic) in
  chat.
- **Code** is written by Claude Code from those specifications and
  reviewed by the author.
- **Every formula is verified** against its cited source before
  implementation.
- **This file is updated at each release.**
